UDPspeeder-simd

A SIMD-accelerated fork of UDPspeeder: a UDP tunnel that improves a lossy, high-latency link with Reed-Solomon forward error correction.

  • Source and releases
  • Throughput benchmarks x86-64
  • Throughput benchmarks PowerPC e500v2, emulated
